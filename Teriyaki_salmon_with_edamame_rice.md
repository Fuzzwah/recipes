# Teriyaki salmon with edamame rice
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fuzzwah/recipes/master/pics/Teriyaki_salmon_with_edamame_rice.jpg)
#### 0:10 Prep -- 0:15 Cook -- 4 Servings
## INGREDIENTS
* 4 (about 150g each) skinless salmon fillets
* 1 cup frozen edamame beans, thawed
* 2 cups jasmine rice
* 2 carrots, peeled, coarsely grated
* 1 Lebanese cucumber, thinly sliced
* 2 green shallots, thinly sliced
* Sesame seeds, to serve
## TERIYAKI SAUCE
* 3 tablespoons soy sauce
* 2 tablespoons honey
* 1 tablespoon rice wine vinegar
* 1 teaspoon sesame oil
* 1 garlic clove, crushed
## METHOD
### Step 1
Cook rice according to packet directions. Stir in edamame and cover to keep warm.
### Step 2
Combine soy sauce, honey, rice wine vinegar, sesame oil and garlic in a small jug to make the teriyaki sauce.
### Step 3
Heat a large non-stick frying pan over medium-high heat. Spray with oil. Cook salmon for 3 minutes each side or until just cooked through. Add teriyaki sauce and simmer for 1-2 minutes, turning salmon to coat.
### Step 4
Divide rice among serving bowls. Top with salmon, carrot and cucumber. Drizzle with remaining sauce from the pan. Scatter with shallots and sesame seeds.
