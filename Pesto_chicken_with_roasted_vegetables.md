# Pesto chicken with roasted vegetables
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fuzzwah/recipes/master/pics/Pesto_chicken_with_roasted_vegetables.jpg)
#### 0:15 Prep -- 0:30 Cook -- 4 Servings
## INGREDIENTS
* 4 (about 200g each) chicken breasts
* 3 tablespoons basil pesto
* 1 head broccoli, cut into small florets
* 2 zucchinis, sliced into rounds
* 1 red capsicum, deseeded, cut into strips
* 250g cherry tomatoes
* 2 tablespoons olive oil
* 2 garlic cloves, crushed
* Pasta or steamed rice, to serve
* Shaved parmesan, to serve
## METHOD
### Step 1
Preheat oven to 200°C/180°C fan-forced. Line two large baking trays with baking paper.
### Step 2
Spread broccoli, zucchini and capsicum in a single layer across the prepared trays. Drizzle with olive oil and scatter with garlic. Season with salt and pepper. Roast for 15 minutes.
### Step 3
Coat chicken breasts in pesto. Add cherry tomatoes to the trays and place chicken on top of the vegetables. Roast for a further 12-15 minutes or until chicken is cooked through and the vegetables are golden.
### Step 4
Serve chicken and vegetables over pasta or steamed rice. Scatter with shaved parmesan.
