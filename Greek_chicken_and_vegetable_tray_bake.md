# Greek chicken and vegetable tray bake
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fuzzwah/recipes/master/pics/Greek_chicken_and_vegetable_tray_bake.jpg)
#### 0:15 Prep -- 0:35 Cook -- 4 Servings
## INGREDIENTS
* 8 (about 1.2kg) chicken thigh cutlets, skin removed
* 2 zucchinis, sliced into rounds
* 1 red capsicum, deseeded, cut into strips
* 1 red onion, cut into wedges
* 250g cherry tomatoes
* 4 garlic cloves, crushed
* 2 tablespoons olive oil
* 1 lemon, rind finely grated, juiced
* 1 teaspoon dried oregano
* Fresh flat-leaf parsley, to serve
* Pita bread or steamed rice, to serve
## METHOD
### Step 1
Preheat oven to 200°C/180°C fan-forced. Line a large baking tray with baking paper.
### Step 2
Combine olive oil, garlic, lemon rind, lemon juice and oregano in a small bowl. Season with salt and pepper.
### Step 3
Arrange chicken on the prepared tray. Scatter zucchini, capsicum, onion and cherry tomatoes around the chicken. Drizzle the olive oil mixture evenly over the chicken and vegetables.
### Step 4
Roast for 35 minutes or until the chicken is cooked through and the vegetables are golden and tender. Scatter with fresh parsley and serve with pita bread or steamed rice.
