# Gianfranco Zola style creamy chicken & sausage
#### 0:15 Prep -- 0:30 Cook -- 4 Servings
## INGREDIENTS
### Proteins:
* 300g chicken breast or thighs, sliced into bite-sized strips
* 2–3 high-quality pork sausages, casings removed and meat crumbled
### The Base:
* 200g brown mushrooms, sliced
* 2 cloves garlic, minced
* 1 small brown onion or shallot, finely diced
### The Sauce:
* 125ml (1/2 cup) dry white wine (e.g., Pinot Grigio, Sauvignon Blanc, or Chardonnay)
* 300ml thickened cream
* 100ml chicken stock
* 1/2 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese
* 1/2 tsp dried oregano or thyme
* Optional: a pinch of red chili flakes
### Pantry/Garnish:
* 1 tbsp olive oil
* Salt and cracked black pepper
* Fresh parsley or basil, chopped
## METHOD
### Step 1 - Brown the Meats
Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the crumbled pork sausage and cook until browned and slightly crispy. Remove with a slotted spoon, leaving the rendered fat in the pan. Add the chicken to the same pan, sear until golden and cooked through, then set aside with the sausage.
### Step 2 - Sauté the Mushrooms
In the same pan, add the mushrooms. Cook until they are browned and have released their moisture. Stir in the onion and garlic, sautéing for another 2 minutes until fragrant.
### Step 3 - Deglaze with Wine
Pour in the dry white wine. Use a wooden spoon to scrape all the browned bits (the fond) off the bottom of the pan—this is where the deep flavor lives. Let the wine simmer for 2–3 minutes until it has reduced by about half.
### Step 4 - Build the Sauce
Stir in the chicken stock, thickened cream, and dried herbs. Bring to a gentle simmer (do not boil rapidly) for 5–7 minutes until the sauce begins to thicken and coat the back of a spoon.
### Step 5 - The "Zola" Finish
Stir in the Parmesan cheese until melted. Return the cooked sausage and chicken (and any juices) to the pan. Toss for 1–2 minutes to heat through and allow the sauce to fully emulsify with the proteins.
### Step 6 - Serve
Taste for seasoning—the wine and Parmesan provide a lot of character, so you may only need a touch of extra pepper. Garnish with fresh herbs.
