# Butter chicken
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fuzzwah/recipes/master/pics/Butter_chicken.jpg)
#### 0:10 Prep -- 0:30 Cook -- 4 Servings
## INGREDIENTS
* 700g (1.5 lb) boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
* 1 tsp salt (for seasoning)
### AROMATICS & SPICES
* 4 cloves garlic, finely minced
* 1 tbsp ginger paste (or freshly grated ginger)
* 2 tsp garam masala
* 1 tsp ground cumin
* 1 tsp ground coriander
* 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper (adjust for heat preference)
### THE SAUCE
* 60g (4 tbsp) unsalted butter, softened
* 1.5 cups (approx. 375ml) tomato puree (passata)
* 1 cup (250ml) heavy cream (full-fat)
### GARNISH
* 1/4 cup fresh cilantro (coriander) leaves, chopped
## METHOD
### Step 1
Pat the chicken pieces dry and toss them with the salt. In a separate small bowl, combine the minced garlic, ginger paste, garam masala, cumin, coriander, and cayenne pepper to create a spice base.
### Step 2
Heat a large, heavy skillet over medium-high heat. Add the softened butter and let it melt until it sizzles. Add the chicken in a single layer and cook for 4–5 minutes without moving them, until golden brown. Flip the pieces and sear the other side. Remove the chicken from the pan and set aside.
### Step 3
Reduce the heat to medium. Add the garlic-ginger-spice mixture to the remaining butter in the pan. Sauté for about 30 seconds until the spices become highly fragrant.
### Step 4
Pour in the tomato puree, using a wooden spoon to scrape up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan. Let the mixture simmer for 8–10 minutes until it darkens and thickens slightly.
### Step 5
Stir in the heavy cream until the sauce is glossy and orange. Return the seared chicken (and any accumulated juices) to the pan. Let everything simmer together gently for another 5–10 minutes to ensure the chicken is tender and the flavors have melded.
### Step 6
Stir in the fresh cilantro just before serving. Taste and add an extra pinch of salt if needed. Let the dish rest for 2–3 minutes before serving to allow the sauce to settle.
